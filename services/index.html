Video Guestbook

More Than a Message. A Memory You Can Relive.

Some memories deserve more than a photograph.

Our Video Guestbook station gives your guests the opportunity to leave heartfelt messages, share funny stories, offer advice, and express their love through personalized video recordings.

With a simple touch of the screen, guests can record a 30–60-second video message with both high quality audio and video.

After your event, you’ll receive a collection of these special recordings to watch, share, and treasure for years to come.

Perfect for weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, corporate celebrations, and special occasions.

What’s included

Every Video Guestbook package includes:

  • Interactive premium Video Guestbook station
  • Video and audio recording
  • 30–60-second guest video messages
  • Simple, guest-friendly recording experience
  • Dedicated on-site attendant
  • Set-up and tear down
  • Delivery of recorded videos to the client after the event

How it works

  1. Walk up

    Guests approach our interactive Video Guestbook station.

  2. Tap & record

    With a simple touch of the screen, guests record a 30–60-second personalized video message with audio.

  3. Relive the memories

    After the event, the client receives the collected video messages to enjoy and revisit.
