About

More than a photo booth. A premium portrait experience.

Our Story

Built for guests who want photos worth keeping.

Butlers PhotoBooth was created to bring a more elevated photo booth experience to events. We wanted to move away from the typical self-serve booth and offer something that feels closer to a real photo studio — but still fun, simple, and perfect for guests.

With professional lighting, clean backdrops, and a live photographer guiding each session, we capture portraits that guests actually want to keep, share, and use as profile photos. Every detail is designed to make people feel comfortable, confident, and well-photographed.

Our goal is simple: to give every event a premium portrait experience that feels polished, personal, and memorable.

Butlers PhotoBooth is not just about taking pictures. It is about creating moments your guests will remember long after the event.

What sets us apart

A studio experience, brought to you.

At Butlers PhotoBooth, we create more than just event photos — we bring a professional portrait studio directly to your celebration. Our experience is designed for guests who want polished, high-quality images that feel modern, elegant, and worth keeping.

A Real Photographer Behind the Camera

Our booth is guided by a professional photographer, not an automated machine. We help guests with posing, angles, and expressions so they feel relaxed and look their best in every shot.

Studio-Quality Setup

We use professional lighting and equipment to create a clean, elevated portrait look. This allows us to capture images with depth, detail, and a premium finish that stands out from a standard photo booth.

Photos Guests Actually Value

Our goal is to create images people are proud to save, share, and even use as profile pictures. Every photo is taken with care, making the final result feel more like a studio portrait than a quick event snapshot.

Designed to Fit Your Event

Whether it’s a wedding, corporate event, private party, or special celebration, our setup blends into the space with a polished and professional look. We focus on making the experience smooth, stylish, and memorable for both hosts and guests.
